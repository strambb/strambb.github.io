# Welcome to my blog and portfolio site!

Nice to have you here. This is my personal blog and site on which I publish everything from personal notes, how-to's as well as tips and tricks I encounter. 
With this blog I want to document things for me personally and hope that maybe some of you find something useful here, too!

After multiple years of product management for digital products I made the transition into the technical area. Currently, I'm gaining experience as a backend developer mainly focusing on API development and handling everything from requirements gathering with stakeholders up to delivery and support on a state-of-the-art tech stack (k8s).

With this position in the product development flow, the largest obstacle is communication between the business and technical sides: developers need clear requirements and decisions on details that have tremendous effects on design and complexity, while business normally thinks in impacts. What outcome can we achieve in which timeframe? Delivering an experience is far more crucial than the underlying details. It basically just needs to run reliably (and fast, secure, stable, with a nice UI, ...).
I like to take up the challenge and try to translate between both sides of the chasm to build a bridge and ensure the best outcome for both sides. Feel free to reach out to me if you look for a part-time tech PO or backend dev who understands the business lingo.

Another interest of mine is Cyber Security and its influence on product design and development. I'm certified Security Champion and as such responsible for the security of certain products. To deepen my understanding of the interplay between offensive attacks and defensive application security measures I'm on my way to obtain the HTB CPTS certification. My progress on this journey can also be found here in multiple blog posts.

Besides that, I am curious about how AI will change the future of knowledge work in general. Seeing the speed of development in my immediate environment and the impact it has on my daily work, I no longer believe the impact will not be profound for a large portion of knowledge workers. I would be happy to hear your take on the topic!
